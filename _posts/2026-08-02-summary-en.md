## [OpenAI's Astra Model Cracks Ten Long-Standing Math Problems](https://openai.com/index/ten-advances-in-mathematics/) ⭐️ 9.0/10

OpenAI announced that an internal version of its next-generation model, Astra, has produced new results on ten long-standing open problems in mathematics and theoretical computer science. The arguments were human-assisted formalized in the Lean proof assistant, with the underlying mathematical reasoning generated by the AI. This marks a landmark demonstration of AI making original research contributions in mathematics, potentially shifting both mathematical practice and AI evaluation. If verified, these results could resolve questions that have resisted progress for decades, including a counterexample to Connes' rigidity conjecture and the existence of non-sofic groups. OpenAI reports that generating each argument cost under $2,000 in tokens at GPT-5.6 Sol prices. The company released Lean 4 formalizations in the openai/ten-proofs GitHub repository, a technical paper, and an LLM-generated PDF reconstructing the reasoning traces.

telegram · zaihuapd · Aug 1, 07:59

**Background**: Lean is an open-source proof assistant and functional programming language used to write and verify mathematical proofs in a formal system. Formalization in Lean ensures that every step of an argument can be mechanically checked by a computer. The targeted problems include Connes' rigidity conjecture, which asks whether certain von Neumann algebras uniquely determine their underlying groups, and the existence of non-sofic groups, a question about whether all groups satisfy a certain finitary approximation property. Other problems, such as sphere packing, Ramsey numbers, and arithmetic circuit lower bounds, are classical topics in geometry, combinatorics, and computational complexity.

A new interactive tour showcases the key changes in Go 1.27, including generics enhancements, runtime fixes, and standard library updates. The tour also highlights a fix making runtime.findnull() compatible with MTE on Android, enabling gomobile apps on systems like GrapheneOS. Go is a widely used programming language, so the changes in Go 1.27 affect many developers and production systems. Notable details include the generics syntax exemplified by `(b Box[T]) Map[U any](f func(T) U) Box[U]`, the MTE compatibility fix for runtime.findnull(), and automatic draining of HTTP response bodies. ## [Open Letters Reveal AI Open-Weight Policy Clash](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Aug/2/open-letters/#atom-everything) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Microsoft shepherded an open letter dated July 24, 2026, signed by 235 AI-adjacent companies including NVIDIA, Amazon, and OpenAI, arguing against US government restrictions on open-weight models. Anthropic declined to sign and published its own position three days later, while a third letter called 'Pacing the Frontier' gathered 1,324 frontier-AI employees requesting governance tools to pace automated AI development. This cluster of open letters shows a rare public rift among leading AI companies over open-weight model regulation, directly influencing US policy debates on AI safety, competition, and national security. The outcome will shape whether open-weight models remain broadly accessible or face new government constraints. The Microsoft-led letter explicitly defends distillation as a legitimate model-development technique, and OpenAI added its signature after the initial publication. Notably absent, Anthropic's CEO Dario Amodei called for cracking down on industrial-scale distillation while stating the company never advocated a ban on open-weights models; the 'Pacing the Frontier' letter focuses on risks from automated AI research and intense competitive pressure.

rss · Simon Willison · Aug 2, 04:16

**Background**: Open-weight models are AI models whose trained parameters are publicly downloadable, allowing anyone to inspect, modify, and run them on their own infrastructure. Governments have grown concerned that such models could be misused for cyberattacks or biological weapons, and the US government recently directed Anthropic to suspend access to its Claude Fable 5 model over 'safety' concerns. Distillation, where one model trains on another model's outputs, is a widely used technique but has become contentious as a potential vector for 'misappropriation'.
