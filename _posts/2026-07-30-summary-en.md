## [Open-source engine runs Gemma 4 26B in 2GB RAM on any M-series Mac](https://github.com/drumih/turbo-fieldfare) ⭐️ 9.0/10

TurboFieldfare, an open-source inference engine written in Swift and Metal, runs a 4-bit quantized Gemma 4 26B-A4B-IT model on any M-series Mac using only ~2 GB RAM by streaming expert weights from SSD. This breakthrough enables running large Mixture-of-Experts models on consumer hardware with limited RAM, democratizing on-device AI and reducing the need for expensive high-memory machines. The engine achieves 5–6 tok/s on an 8 GB M2 MacBook Air and 31–35 tok/s on an M5 MacBook Pro, using a small expert cache and bounded parallel pread system calls to hide SSD latency.

hackernews · gitpusher42 · Jul 29, 15:05 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49098510)

**Background**: Gemma 4 26B is a Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) language model where only a subset of parameters (experts) are activated per token. Traditional inference requires loading all 14 GB of 4-bit quantized weights into RAM, which exceeds typical consumer Mac memory. TurboFieldfare keeps the shared core and KV cache in RAM and streams only the routed experts from SSD, exploiting MoE sparsity to drastically reduce memory usage.

## [AI Finds Critical Weakness in NIST Post-Quantum Candidate HAWK](https://startupfortune.com/claude-mythos-broke-hawk-and-the-nist-post-quantum-timeline-may-not-survive-it/) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Anthropic's Claude Mythos Preview AI identified a severe weakness in HAWK-256, a NIST post-quantum digital signature candidate, reducing its effective key strength from 2^64 to 2^38 in about 60 hours at a cost of ~$100,000 in API fees. This event demonstrates that AI can now outperform human cryptanalysts in finding weaknesses in cryptographic algorithms, potentially accelerating the post-quantum standardization process and forcing earlier adoption of crypto-agility. It also raises questions about the security of other NIST candidates. The attack is not polynomial-time, so larger HAWK parameters remain secure; HAWK has not been withdrawn publicly. Anthropic also reported an improved attack on 7-round AES-128, but full AES-128 (10 rounds) is unaffected. The research used the Claude Mythos Preview model, which is only available to select partners.

telegram · zaihuapd · Jul 30, 05:47

**Background**: Post-quantum cryptography aims to develop algorithms resistant to quantum computers that could break current encryption. NIST has been running a competition to select post-quantum standards; HAWK is a candidate digital signature scheme in the third round. Cryptographic agility is the ability to switch between algorithms without major disruption, which is crucial for migrating to post-quantum systems.
